# Visual direction — manga x Chog
Concept: the human world was drained of its memory and color. Chog awakens as vibrant violet re-enters the page. A Chog-native UI frames an authored monochrome adventure manga. Never reskin the original collectible or impersonate its metadata.

Palette: deep ink #161220, Chog violet #794CFA, paper #F8F3E8, lilac #E5D5FF, warm pink #FF7FC7. These are OUR proposed colors, not official hex codes.
- UI: expressive rounded buttons, off-white reading surfaces, sharp hierarchy and whimsical microcopy.
- Panels: dramatic black-and-white inking, character continuity, legible dialogue and carefully constrained animation. No generic AI neon tunnel, black-purple blob backgrounds or slideshow transitions.
- Neri: 19-year-old apprentice archivist, dark short hair, practical uniform, worn satchel.
- Pip: small mischievous Chog-spirit; resembles Chog cartoon language without pretending to be a token's actual artwork.
- Sera: strict redactor captain, sharp silhouettes, intimidating controlled posture.
- Rook: smiling harbor smuggler, fast-talking and disarming.
- Create a character model sheet first; do not generate every panel independently with inconsistent faces.
- Before calling it a manga, replace stage glyph placeholders with legitimate narrative illustrations and at least two interactive illustrated moments.
- First artwork batch: (1) illustrated cover, (2) Veyra archive wide shot, (3) Neri close-up, (4) Pip first reveal. Approval gate here.
- Then: drawer, inspectors, Redactor chase, memory seal, ruined sea glimpse, harbor, photograph, final surveillance panel.
- Optimize images to webp/avif with accessible fallbacks, mobile aspect ratios. Audio is opt-in and never blocks reading.

## CANONICAL NFT CHARACTER RULE — PRIORITY OVERRIDE
**All Chog characters appearing as surviving seals in the manga MUST be actual Chog Genesis NFTs, backed by verifiable Monad token IDs and genuine unmodified artwork/metadata.** Do not fabricate random Chog-like creatures for the cast or claim generated art belongs to the collection. Make the collection recognizable. Characters are discovered by the player using authentic media, and the actual token's traits inform the invented in-universe role. 

Human cast (Neri, Sera, Rook), locations, scenery, props, and Chog's speculative story roles are original illustrations. The introductory companion 'Pip' is a fictional **nickname/role** for one selected real Genesis NFT; do not draw an arbitrary substitute and label it Pip. Select a real token with verified onchain token ID, attributes and existing art before completing Pip's design, and record the provenance + reason for the selection. If no real verified token is selected yet, keep Pip's representation as a clearly labeled placeholder rather than fabricated NFT art.

To animate a Chog: use motion, panel framing, overlays and appropriately credited interpretations that retain the NFT's recognizable appearance. Distinguish scene illustrations from the unmodified original collectible. Do not use an NFT holder's identity or imply official creator endorsement. Preserve authentic character personality and playfulness; manga styling applies mainly to surrounding humans, backgrounds and dramatic compositions.
